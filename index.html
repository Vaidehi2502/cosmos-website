The Sun

A 4.6-billion-year-old G-type star holding 99.8% of the Solar System's mass and fusing 600 million tonnes of hydrogen every second.

Magic fact: Light leaving the Sun's core can take 100,000 years to reach its surface.

Study stars in the library

Earth

The only world known to host life, wrapped in a nitrogen-rich atmosphere and shielded by a powerful magnetic field.

Magic fact: Earth travels around the Sun at roughly 107,000 kilometres per hour.

Compare Earth in the lab

Saturn

A gas giant famous for its dazzling ring system of ice and rock, orbited by more than 140 confirmed moons.

Magic fact: Saturn is so light it would float in a bathtub of water large enough to hold it.
